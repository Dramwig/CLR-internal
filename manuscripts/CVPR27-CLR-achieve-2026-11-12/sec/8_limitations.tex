\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}
Readers are trained on problems the source models were trained on and evaluated on unseen problems; the resulting shift lowers accuracy by 5--11 points for probes and we have not removed it. The LLaMA value labels are highly degenerate (14.8\% non-degenerate), so its Q1 results are weaker evidence. Message auditing is evaluated on synthetic episodes with programmatic commitments and one training-free relay channel; HiddenBench was too small to support conclusions, and learned latent channels (e.g.\ C2C) are untested. The auditor reads lexical content and does not detect honest reasoning errors. Conformal guarantees hold only under exchangeability and do not cover adaptive perturbations. Huginn and Coconut were not evaluated because of compute.
